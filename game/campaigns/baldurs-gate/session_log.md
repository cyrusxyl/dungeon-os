## Session 2 Events

### The Negotiation
- Sireth rolled Insight check (1d20+2 = 11) to read Cassara
- Result: Couldn't tell if hiding anything specific (needed DC 14+)
- Sireth: "I can help you, but not for free. What can you offer?"
- **Cassara offered**: 30 gold + her knowledge of Council Hall (layout, schedules, contacts)

### The Deal
- Sireth accepted and took 30 gold
- Cassara revealed: Sister Gareth at Chapel of Ilmater (Dock Ward) has independent evidence
- Warning: Half the Dock Ward are Flaming Fist informants; don't use same safe house

### The Journey
- Sireth traveled south from Marketplace Quarter to Dock Ward
- Found Chapel of Ilmater (symbol: bound hands, god of suffering) by harbor stairs
- Sister Gareth present, arranging altar offerings
- She recognized Sireth immediately: "What brings a drow monk to this particular chapel?"
