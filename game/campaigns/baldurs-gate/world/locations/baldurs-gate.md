# Baldur's Gate

## Overview
Baldur's Gate is a bustling metropolis on the Sword Coast, known as the "City of Blood" due to its violent history. It serves as a crucial trading hub between the Sword Coast and lands to the east and south. The city is divided into three main sections: the Upper City, Lower City, and Outer City.

## Geography

### Upper City
The wealthy and influential make their homes here, behind the high walls that separate them from the chaos below. The Upper City contains:
- High Hall (seat of government)
- Temples to major deities
- Estates of patriar families (nobility)
- The Wide (a large market square)

### Lower City
The commercial heart of Baldur's Gate, a dense warren of shops, taverns, and warehouses:
- Harbor and docks
- Major markets and trading houses
- The Flaming Fist mercenary company headquarters
- Countless taverns and inns
- Residential districts

### Outer City
Outside the main walls, a sprawling collection of shanties, warehouses, and businesses too dangerous or too poor for the walled city:
- Basilisk Gate (main eastern entrance)
- Blackgate (western entrance)
- Tumbledown district
- Warehouse district
- Refugee camps

## Power Structure

### The Council of Four
- The Dukes who govern the city
- Grand Duke Ulder Ravengard (Flaming Fist commander)
- Three other Dukes from patriar families

### The Flaming Fist
- Powerful mercenary company serving as city watch
- Over 1,500 active members
- Known for brutal efficiency

### The Guild
- Criminal organization controlling much of the underground economy
- Led by mysterious figures known as "Kingpins"
- Operates from the shadows

### Patriar Families
- Nobility who control trade, land, and politics
- Ancient bloodlines with deep roots in the city

## Notable Locations

### Elfsong Tavern
Famous for its ghostly singing, a popular gathering place for adventurers.

### Baldur's Mouth Gazette
The city's newspaper, often used for propaganda by whoever can pay.

### Sorcerous Sundries
Premier magic shop in the Lower City.

### Wyrm's Rock Fortress
Ancient fortress on an island in the harbor, home to the Flaming Fist.

## Current Atmosphere
Autumn is settling over the city. Trade caravans arrive daily from the east. Tensions simmer between the various factions. The Guild and Flaming Fist wage a quiet war in the shadows. Refugees from the countryside seek safety behind the walls, only to find themselves trapped in the Outer City's squalor.

Adventure calls to those brave (or foolish) enough to answer.
