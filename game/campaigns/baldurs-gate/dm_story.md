# DM Story Bible — Baldur's Gate Campaign
<!-- DM EYES ONLY — Player should not read this during active play -->

## Core Premise
A drow monk folk hero arrives in Baldur's Gate and stumbles into a web of institutional corruption — the Flaming Fist, the city's mercenary police force, is running a protection racket with slave traders and silencing anyone who threatens to expose them. The player must decide how far to push back against a system that has the city in its grip.

**Central Theme**: Power vs. Justice — can one outsider make a dent against entrenched institutions, and at what cost?

---

## The Real Conspiracy (Full Picture)

### The Slave Trade Pipeline
- **The Flaming Fist** (specifically Commander Voss and a network of corrupt officers) takes bribes from a slave trading ring operating out of the docks
- Slaves move through Baldur's Gate disguised as "indentured servants" or cargo
- The merchants who run the pipeline: **House Maren** (a respected trading family, publicly charitable)
- The route: Underdark procurement → Baldur's Gate staging → Amn markets

### The Murder of Aldus Thorne
- Thorne was a merchant who accidentally discovered the pipeline while competing for dock contracts
- He brought evidence to the Council of Four — but **Councillor Duvane** (seemingly reform-minded) is also on the Flaming Fist payroll
- Duvane tipped off Voss; the assassination contract followed
- Cassara Whitmore (Duvane's scribe) copied the contract before fleeing

### The Bigger Cover-Up
- **Half the Council** is compromised to varying degrees — some bribed, some blackmailed
- The Thieves' Guild *knows* about the pipeline but has a non-interference pact with Voss (they stay out of each other's business)
- The Harper network has a sleeper agent in the city but hasn't moved yet — waiting for actionable proof

## Story Branches

### Branch A — The Legal Path (Low Risk, Slow Burn)
Player attempts to expose through legitimate channels: Council Hall archives, sympathetic magistrates, the press (a broadsheet called *The Baldur's Mouth*).

- **If pursued**: Council is slow, dangerous, Duvane will catch on and try to neutralize Cassara and Sireth quietly
- **Key NPC unlock**: Magistrate Lorn — honest but cowardly, needs to be convinced to act
- **Endpoint**: Partial justice — Voss falls, Duvane survives, pipeline disrupted but not destroyed

### Branch B — The Shadow Path (High Risk, Decisive)
Player works through back channels: the Thieves' Guild, blackmail, breaking into Flaming Fist HQ, Underdark contacts.

- **If pursued**: The Guild will demand a favor in return (a future hook). Voss will escalate to open violence.
- **Key NPC unlock**: Mira Ashcroft — Guild fixer, morally grey, respects competence
- **Endpoint**: Voss and Duvane both fall, but player is now indebted to the Guild

### Branch C — The Harper Path (Medium Risk, Broader Stakes)
Sister Gareth is secretly a Harper agent. If player earns her trust, she brings the network in.

- **If pursued**: Raises the stakes to a regional level — the pipeline is just one node in a larger Amnian operation
- **Key NPC unlock**: A Harper contact in Waterdeep gets involved, quest expands beyond Baldur's Gate
- **Endpoint**: Full pipeline destroyed, major political upheaval, Sireth gets Harper attention (positive or complicating)

### Branch D — Derailment (Player goes off-script)
Player ignores the main quest, pursues mercenary work, etc.

- **Keep the world alive**: Cassara gets caught in 2 sessions if Sireth doesn't act. This creates a moral weight — the world moves without them.
- Voss will eventually notice that someone copied the contract and start hunting

## Key NPCs (Full Profiles)

### Commander Voss
- Former war hero, genuinely believed he was protecting the city by "controlling" the slave trade rather than letting chaos reign
- Now fully corrupted; rationalizes it as necessary evil
- **Weakness**: His genuine loyalty to his troops — a soldier dying because of his choices could crack him

### Captain Harwin
- Voss's enforcer; does the dirty work; no illusions about what they're doing; purely mercenary
- **Weakness**: Has a daughter in the Lower City he's been sending money to

### Councillor Duvane
- Believes the system is too broken to fix from outside; chose to survive inside it
- Not evil, just exhausted and compromised; genuinely hates what he's become
- **Weakness**: Still wants to be the reformer he once was — can be turned if given a way out

### Sister Gareth
- Harper agent, uses the chapel as cover; genuine faith, not cynical
- Will not reveal herself until she trusts Sireth completely (Insight DC 16 or deliberate approach)
- **Weakness**: Her Harper mission may conflict with what's best for Cassara personally

### Cassara Whitmore
- Braver than she looks; has been hiding for three weeks alone
- Starting to crack; if Sireth doesn't return within a session she'll try to run on her own (badly)
- **Secret**: She made a second copy of the contract; she hasn't told Sireth yet

## Sireth-Specific Hooks

- **Drow Identity**: The Flaming Fist has standing orders to detain "suspicious non-surface races." This creates ongoing friction — Sireth can't operate openly without papers or cover.
- **Underdark Network**: Sireth's background could be a resource OR a liability. House Maren's slave pipeline *goes through the Underdark* — Sireth may recognize names or routes.
- **Folk Hero Background**: Someone in the city has heard of Sireth's deeds on the surface. A potential ally or complication.
