# Setting shelf (own words, summary level)

## The city `[old]`
Baldur's Gate is a port city on the Sword Coast. Trade made it rich. Parts:
- **Lower City**: south of the old wall, between the wall and the Chionthar river. Docks, markets, poor streets, thieves. Most street-level play starts here.
- **Upper City**: behind the wall. Rich homes, temples, guild halls.
- **Outer City**: outside the walls. Rough, crowded, hard to police. Refugees camp here in hard years.
- **Wyrm's Crossing and Wyrm's Rock**: a bridge over the Chionthar, with a Flaming Fist fortress on an islet at its center. It links the Lower City to the camp at **Rivington** on the far bank. The Fist takes a toll there.

Rule: four Grand Dukes and a council. Their power depends on trade and the Flaming Fist. `[1480s]` Names and loyalties differ from `[1492]`. Check `dm_story.md`.

## Law and groups `[old]`
- **Flaming Fist**: mercenary army that acts as the city's military and police. Open to bribes and politics at the top.
- **City Watch**: small civil force. Often outranked by the Fist.
- **Harpers**: secret network that opposes tyrants.
- **Zhentarim**: black-market trade and mercenaries.
- **Emerald Enclave**: nature wardens.
- **Lords' Alliance**: rulers' coalition.
- **Thieves' guilds and gangs**: local, many, shifting.

## Gods `[old]`
- **The Dead Three**: Bane (tyranny), Bhaal (murder), Myrkul (death). They died once and want to return. Their cults are old and patient.
- **Shar** (loss, night) and **Selûne** (the moon) are twin goddess rivals. Their servants fight over small things and large.
- **Mystra** (magic, the Weave). Her good will matters to wizards.
- **Mizora, Zariel, Mephistopheles, Asmodeus** are devils. Pacts with them cost far more than they give.
- **Lolth** rules the drow. **Gith / Vlaakith** rule the githyanki.

## Underlying threats
- **Mind flayers and the Elder Brain** `[1492]`: tadpoles in the brain, a colony that builds an army. In `[1480s]` rumors only, if at all.
- **The Absolute and the Netherstones** `[1492]`: a hidden cult uses a false god to gather an army. Three Netherstones can control the Elder Brain. Do not show these before the campaign date unless `dm_story.md` moves them.
- **Cult of Bhaal** `[old, grows 1492]`: murder cult with a hidden Chosen. DM-ONLY: its Chosen plans a blood offering to the city.
- **Vampire spawn and masters** `[1480s]`: a lord in the Upper City keeps spawn in secret (this campaign's core).
- **Underdark and old ruins** `[old]`: gnomish tunnels, drowned temples, vaults older than the city.
